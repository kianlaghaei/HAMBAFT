# بازار هزارچراغ — vertical slice 0.1.0

`stories/hezar-cheragh/0.1.0` is a 25–40 minute Persian development slice for 2–4 Teams. It is not the full game. The market opens with «امروز زنگ بازار به صدا درنیامد.» and the disappearance of حاج صادق. Four businesses receive different ledger fragments and pressures:

- `bakery-sepideh` — نانوایی سپیده: inventory, price, promise and local trust;
- `logistics-rah-no` — باربری راه‌نو: routes, capacity, safety and gatekeeping;
- `printing-roshan` — چاپخانه روشن: conflicting evidence, paid influence and transparency;
- `exchange-mizan` — صرافی میزان: credit, guarantees, liquidity and control.

The four internal checkpoints are صبح بی‌زنگ, بار نرسید, پیشنهاد آوان and جمع‌شدن بازار. آوان («گروه سرمایه‌گذاری و بازآفرینی شهری آوان»، «شهر، دوباره جریان پیدا می‌کند.») offers material benefits paired with pricing, route, publication or data-control costs. It is written as a plausible institution, not a caricature.

The package uses `emergency-supply`, `credit-guarantee`, `evidence-exchange`, `public-announcement` and `shared-purchase` structured interactions. Delayed reviews cover a stable-price promise, hidden route, unattributed notice and emergency credit; at least one triggered consequence participates in Ending evidence/eligibility.

## Team counts and uncontrolled businesses

With two Teams, two businesses are assigned deterministic AuthoredBehavior profiles; with three, one is assigned; with four, none is required. Eligible profiles are `community-first`, `opportunistic`, `defensive`, `avan-aligned` and `debt-pressured`. Selection and actions derive from package hash, seed, difficulty and stable Entity identity. Hard difficulty increases weights for internally rational self-interested rules; it does not introduce runtime AI or wall-clock randomness.

## Endings

There are three directional Entity Endings per business (12 total), four normal World Endings and the difficult development ending `the-open-ledger` / «دفتر باز». Entity Endings describe the direction established by this slice, not the final fate of a future long campaign. World fallbacks guarantee completion, while strong transparency and disclosed evidence can unlock دفتر باز.

## Validated path

Automated 2-, 3- and 4-Team scenarios load the package, create all businesses, select uncontrolled profiles, initialize Ink, submit private choices, resolve all checkpoints, trigger consequences and atomically resolve dual Endings. The primary scenario additionally sends, counters and accepts an immutable Proposal revision, executes its Agreement and expires a different Proposal. Same-seed runs compare Storylet assignments, Ink refs, behavior Actions, expiration, Agreement/Consequence state, Ending IDs and final fingerprint.

Content is intentionally bounded: one contemporary market morning-to-gathering arc, Persian only, no final «هزارچراغ» hidden ending, and no multi-hour continuation.
