# Diegetic market experience

The Team page is organized around time/atmosphere, the living market, a progressive context panel and a compact market/business pulse. Narrative and decisions remain immediately available; visual loading never gates commands.

## Narrative time

The authored vocabulary is `before-open`, `morning`, `near-noon`, `afternoon`, `dusk`, `night`. The opening tour supplies the before-open spatial introduction. Runtime scenes restore morning at `morning-without-bell`, near noon at `cargo-did-not-arrive`, afternoon at `avan-offer`, dusk at `market-gathering`, and night at `slice-complete`. Scene data controls light, lantern, shadow, crowd, opening/closing, soundscape, courtyard, messenger and Avan tags. It never reads the device clock.

## Interaction

Location hotspots signal quiet, new/action, proposal, agreement, consequence, public-event, changed and ending relevance without constant flashing. Inspection memory is local-only. Relationship lines appear only for a selected related business or active Agreement. Proposal creation offers a spatial business target surface plus a native select fallback and shows context before submission.

Choices remain ordinary authoritative HAMBAFT commands. Package metadata changes their presentation into a document, ledger, notice, cargo board, route, seal or allocation card. Known cost/risk and unknown-consequence markers are authored; hidden Effects are never serialized. Evidence `<details>` is inspect-only and has no mutation handler.

## World reaction

After a newer semantic projection arrives, one to three package-authored reaction lines are shown for four seconds each, with affected locations encoded in the projection. The sequence is skippable, remembered by visual version, and reduced-motion retains the text without requiring animation. React never composes outcome prose.

## Fallback and performance

Initial React narrative renders independently of lazy Pixi. SVG/CSS fallback contains the same semantic time, location list, pulse, character context, reactions and actions. Pixi cleanup removes canvas and resize listener. SignalR remains a refetch hint and centralized handler owner. Ambient events are deterministic, public-safe and capped at three authored selections; renderer diagnostics cap the visual pool at 24. Offscreen/audio work is optional; audio starts only after a user action.

Current limitation: the generated vector market has semantic variations but no final illustrated asset set; prior-band trend persistence and real spatial camera tweening beyond the guided CSS pan remain future polish.
